**Valve-Sizing-Calculator**

A Python-based tool for valve sizing in chemical engineering applications. The calculator determines the required valve flow coefficient (Cv) and appropriate valve size for both liquid and gas services under various process conditions.

The tool is customizable — users can enter different fluid properties (density, viscosity, molecular weight) and operating conditions (flow rate, pressure drop, temperature) to adapt it for real plant design and troubleshooting.

**Features**

Cv and valve size calculation for liquids, gases, and vapors
Supports different valve types (globe, ball, butterfly, etc.)
Accepts custom fluid and operating conditions
Exports results to CSV/Excel for documentation
Visualization of flow vs Cv using matplotlib

**Use cases**

Process and plant design
Valve selection in pilot plants and R&D setups
Teaching & demonstration in chemical/process engineering
Quick checks for engineers in the field
